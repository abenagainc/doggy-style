# Document 13 — Content & Messaging Specification

Doggy Style — Product Architecture | Draft v0.2

- Voice: clear, friendly, trustworthy, dog-first and direct.

- Primary actions: Pass, Interested, Strong Interest, View Profile, Continue, Start Conversation, Proceed, End Connection, Block, Report, Review Passed Dogs, Edit Preferences.

- Prefer dog-specific match language such as 'Max and Luna are interested in each other.'

- Normal interest: 'Interest sent.' Strong interest: 'Strong Interest sent.'

- Discovery exhaustion explains the state and offers Review Passed Dogs and Edit Preferences.

- Verification labels precisely describe what was verified.

- Safety copy clearly explains consequences before block/report confirmation.

- Dog-specific notifications identify the relevant dog.
