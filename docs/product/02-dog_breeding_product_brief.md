Document 02 — Dog Breeding Product Brief

Doggy Style — Initial Product Rollout


## 1. Product Vision

- Create a dog-first matching application where owners create and manage one or more dog profiles and discover potential breeding partners for their dogs.

- The application should feel like a platform for managing and matching dogs rather than an owner-centric social network.


## 2. Primary Matching Model

- Primary matching entity: Dog ↔ Dog.

- Human relationship: Owner ↔ Owner.

- The dog is the visible and primary product identity; the owner is the account holder and operator.


## 3. Multi-Dog Model

- One owner can manage multiple independent dog profiles.

- Each dog has its own discovery, interests, connections, conversations, preferences, availability, and profile information.

- An Active Dog determines the dog-specific context of the main application.


## 4. Core Product Loop

- Create account → create dog → complete breeding profile → activate dog → discover → express interest → mutual interest → connection → chat/screening → proceed or close.


## 5. Product Philosophy

- Dog-first experience.

- Owner-managed account.

- Easy switching between dogs.

- Dog-specific matching and relationship context.

- Owner-level account and security controls kept separate from dog-level information.
