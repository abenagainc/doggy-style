Document 03 — Product Requirements

Doggy Style — MVP Requirements


## 1. Owner Account

- Users can create an account, authenticate, verify their account, manage personal information, and manage privacy/security settings.


## 2. Dog Management

- Users can create one or more dogs.

- Users can maintain dog profiles including photos, basic information, health, pedigree, temperament, breeding information, preferences, and availability.

- Dog profile completeness and eligibility determine whether a dog can participate in discovery.


## 3. Discovery & Matching

- Discovery is scoped to the Active Dog.

- Users can review candidate dog profiles.

- Users can pass or express interest.

- Interest is directional and does not itself create a connection.

- Mutual interest creates a connection.


## 4. Connections & Conversations

- Connections are dog-specific and represent a relationship between the two owners around their dogs.

- Conversations belong to connections.

- Chat is the screening environment where owners can exchange information and decide whether to proceed.


## 5. Decisions

- Proceeding is separate from matching.

- Both owners should explicitly confirm before a connection enters a proceeding/agreement state.

- Either party can end a connection.


## 6. Multi-Dog UX

- An Active Dog is globally accessible.

- Switching dogs changes the context of discovery, connections, and messages.

- Owner-level areas can aggregate information across all dogs.


## 7. Safety

- Reporting and blocking are available from relevant dog, owner, and conversation contexts.

- Blocking operates at the owner level.
