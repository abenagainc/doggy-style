Document 04 — Information Architecture

Doggy Style — Dog-First Architecture


## 1. IA Objective

- The application should make the dog the primary visible and matching entity while keeping the owner as the underlying identity and control layer.


## 2. Primary Hierarchy

- Owner Account → Account / Dogs.

- Each dog has Profile, Breeding Preferences, Discover, Connections, and Conversations.

- Dog-specific activity is scoped to the Active Dog.


## 3. Active Dog

- The Active Dog is the dog currently selected by the owner.

- It is a global application context that determines which dog's discovery, connections, conversations, profile, preferences, and availability are being managed.


## 4. Top-Level Areas

- Discover — primary acquisition and matching experience.

- Connections — mutual-interest relationships.

- Messages — conversations attached to connections.

- My Dogs — dog management.

- Account/Settings — owner-level management.


## 5. Information Ownership

- Dog-level: photos, breed, age, sex, health, pedigree, temperament, breeding preferences, availability, connections, matching activity.

- Owner-level: name, email, phone, identity, account verification, security, privacy, subscription, notifications.

- Connection-level: the two dogs, the two owners, connection status, conversation, screening, decision, and agreement.


## 6. IA Principles

- Dog-first.

- Owner-managed.

- Active Dog.

- Easy switching.

- Context preservation.

- Separation of dog, owner, and connection information.
