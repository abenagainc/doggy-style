# Document 17 — Wireframe Specification

Doggy Style — Product Architecture | Draft v0.2

- Objective: translate approved architecture into low-fidelity layouts before visual design or implementation.

- Rules: dog-first hierarchy; Active Dog visible; Pass/Interested/Strong Interest prominent; candidate decisions possible without excessive navigation; states designed explicitly.

- Wireframe families: authentication, onboarding, dog creation/completion, switcher, Discover, candidate detail, interests, mutual interest, connections, conversation, proceeding, My Dogs, verification, notifications, settings, safety.

- Discover wireframe: Active Dog context, large candidate card, identity, distance, verification, compatibility signals, Pass/Interested/Strong Interest actions, full-profile path.

- Conversation wireframe: dog-pair header, message stream, composer, share profile, proceed/end/report/block, system lifecycle events.

- Every major family includes loading, empty, error, success, incomplete, unavailable and relevant verification/permission states.

- Prototype order: golden path first, then multi-dog switching and exception states.
